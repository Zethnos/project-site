FAQs & Policies

What is the standard turnaround?

Standard turnaround: 3 to 5 business days after payment clears and all required materials are received.

How many revisions are included?

One reasonable revision round for a mismatch with the agreed scope. New features and changed requirements are quoted separately.

What about follow-ups after delivery?

Two email follow-ups within seven calendar days of delivery, limited to the work delivered.

What operating systems are supported?

Windows-first service. Other operating systems may be assessed by request, without implying standard support.

Do you need remote access to my computer?

Customer-controlled screen sharing only. Remote control is not included by default; you retain control of your computer.

What should I never send?

Never send passwords, stream keys, account recovery codes, authentication tokens, or similar secrets. Share redacted screenshots or configuration exports where possible.

Are third-party plugins or hardware included?

Third-party plugins, APIs, and services can change. Paid plugins, hardware, and subscriptions are not included unless quoted.

Is every issue guaranteed to be fixed?

No guarantee that every technical issue can be repaired.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellation before work begins: refund available on request. After work begins, a refund for the uncompleted portion may be available where reasonably calculable, subject to applicable law. Raise delivery concerns by email.
