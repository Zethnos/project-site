How It Works

  1. Email your goal and relevant setup information to business@zethnos.com.
  2. Clarify scope and agree on a price before any work begins.
  3. Payment is arranged through PayPal or Ko-fi after scope approval. I confirm the available payment method and next steps, and work begins only after payment clears and all required materials have been received.

Expectations & Privacy

  • Windows-first service. Other operating systems may be assessed by request, without implying standard support.
  • Email-first and asynchronous by default. Standard turnaround: 3 to 5 business days after payment clears and all required materials are received.
  • One reasonable revision round for a mismatch with the agreed scope. New features and changed requirements are quoted separately.
  • Two email follow-ups within seven calendar days of delivery, limited to the work delivered.
  • Share redacted screenshots or configuration exports where possible. Never send passwords, stream keys, account recovery codes, authentication tokens, or similar secrets. Remote access is not included by default; you retain control of your computer.
  • Third-party plugins, APIs, and services can change. Paid plugins, hardware, and subscriptions are not included unless quoted.
  • No guarantee that every technical issue can be repaired.
  • Cancellation before work begins: refund available on request. After work begins, a refund for the uncompleted portion may be available where reasonably calculable, subject to applicable law. Raise delivery concerns by email.
